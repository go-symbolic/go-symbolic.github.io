What go-symbolic will cover

computer algebra, in pure Go.

go-symbolic is the planned home of computer algebra for go-scikit: expressions, polynomials, differentiation, integration, series, limits and solving, as Mathematica's core offers them, and what Maple adds on top (code generation, algebraic numbers, ODE and PDE solving, triangular decomposition, differential algebra, symbolic random variables, groups and tensors). It is in wave 4, the last of the roadmap, because nearly everything in it waits for the core: the expression DAG, polynomials, GCD and factorisation. Everything below is planned, not released.

∑

Expressions and polynomials

A hash-consed expression DAG with canonical forms, polynomials over Z, Q and F_p, rational functions, differentiation and expansion.

÷

GCD and factorisation

Modular GCD, Cantor-Zassenhaus over F_p, Hensel lifting with van Hoeij recombination over Z.

∫

Integration, series, limits

Rational integration (Hermite, Lazard-Rioboo-Trager), Risch-Norman, a rule engine with Rubi's rules, power series and Gruntz limits.

⚙

Gröbner and solving

Buchberger then F4 modulo p, and polynomial-system solving.

⌘

Code generation to Go

Expressions to Go source with common-subexpression elimination on the DAG; C and Fortran emitters only as test oracles.

∂

The Maple delta

Algebraic numbers, ODE and PDE solving, triangular decomposition and CAD, differential algebra, symbolic random variables, groups and tensors.

🧪

Validated against free oracles

SymPy, python-flint, PARI/GP, Maxima and FriCAS are run as oracles from Go; Maple is proprietary and never one.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

GPL and LGPL systems are compared against, never ported; SymPy, FriCAS, Reduce and Rubi are the portable references.
